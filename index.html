How I work

Before you automate a process or add AI to it, someone has to define it, clean the data and set the controls.

Understand the work

I start with the people who do the job. Interviews and walkthroughs turn into a current-state map and a list of pain points.

Let the data talk

I pull the exports, reconcile them across systems and flag the exceptions, so decisions rest on facts, not guesses.

Define it and prove it

Requirements are written so they can be tested, and every control leaves evidence an auditor can follow.

What I'm building

Founder & builder

Rolevara

Experience the role. Master the work.

Rolevara is a job simulator for security, IT and business roles. Instead of watching videos, learners do the actual job at a fictional company, Pacific Crest Logistics. They work real-looking tickets, calls and alerts, and they're graded on outcome, process and evidence, so they walk into the interview having done the work.

Visit rolevara.com →
  • IAM shift: a live ticket queue of joiners, leavers, MFA resets and urgent lockouts. A ticket that isn't really fixed reopens, and missed work comes back later as an incident.
  • GRC audit: audit a shift as the internal auditor, from walkthrough and sampling to control testing, findings and risk ratings.
  • Real frameworks: tasks map to NIST 800-53, ISO 27001, SOC 2, HIPAA and PCI DSS.
  • More tracks: SOC alerts, PAM, help desk calls and platform labs in Microsoft Entra and AWS.
